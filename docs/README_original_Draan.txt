Localizer(TM): Translate your XMB!
by Draan, 2011

This program is dedicated to PSP-Team.pl community  
You are free to redistribute it, as long as you don't remove this information

*What does it do?
If your language is not officially supported by PSP, you can use this to translate it. It doesn't touch any files to do it, so you can use your favourite ctf/f0 theme, you can switch between themes, and everything will be always translated!

*How do I use it?
Put localizer.prx and localizer.dat into ms0:/seplugins (or ef0:/seplugins) folder, and add proper line to vsh.txt

*How I can obtain/create localizer.dat?
I wrote a small guide on /talk here: http://wololo.net/talk/viewtopic.php?f=26&t=3073
